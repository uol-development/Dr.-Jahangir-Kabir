Who Is Dr. Jahangir Kabir? A Physician On A Natural-Healing Mission

Born in 1974 into a respected Muslim family in Kachua, Bagerhat, Muhammad Jahangir Kabir (known as Dr. Jahangir Kabir) spent his childhood in various cantonments due to his father’s service in the Bangladesh Army. After completing SSC from Shaheed Ramiz Uddin High School (1990), HSC with star marks from Adamjee Cantonment College (1992), and MBBS from Chittagong Medical College (2000), he served as RMO at National Hospital, Feni (2002–2019) before founding the JK Lifestyle movement.

By combining nutrient-dense organic foods with intermittent fasting (Autophagy research in NEJM), the body recycles damaged cellular components, reverses insulin resistance, reduces inflammation, and restores metabolic vitality under medical supervision.
