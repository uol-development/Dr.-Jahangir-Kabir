About Dr. Jahangir Kabir: A Doctor's Training Behind The Movement

Born in 1974 in Kachua Upazila of Bagerhat District, Bangladesh, Dr. Muhammad Jahangir Kabir (ডা. জাহাঙ্গীর কবির) observed throughout decades of clinical practice that chronic illnesses like Type-2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Fatty Liver, Obesity, and PCOS were driven primarily by daily lifestyle habits rather than a lack of medication.

Key Facts About Dr. Jahangir Kabir & Root-Cause Healing

Frequently Asked Questions About Dr. Jahangir Kabir

Muhammad Jahangir Kabir (known as Dr. Jahangir Kabir / ডা. জাহাঙ্গীর কবির) is a Bangladeshi physician, lifestyle specialist, and Founder & Chairman of JK Lifestyle. He earned his MBBS from Chittagong Medical College in 2000 and completed specialized credentials in Ultrasonography (BIUR), Diabetology (CCD, BIRDEM), Asthma, Diabetes & COPD (England), and Spirometry (USA).

The five pillars are: (1) Balanced Diet of organic, natural whole foods; (2) Autophagy through intermittent or regular fasting; (3) Regular Exercise and strength training; (4) Adequate Sleep (going to bed between 9–10 PM and waking early); and (5) Mental Peace.

Autophagy ("self-eating") is the body's natural cellular recycling mechanism that clears out damaged proteins and regenerates healthy cells during fasting windows. Combined with a low-glycemic organic diet, it helps improve insulin sensitivity and immunity.
